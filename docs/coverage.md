# Coverage

Every construct of P4's core, walked in the order of P4-SpecTec's
elaborated IL, with one of three statuses:

- **in**: the construct exists in the IR as itself;
- **elaborated**: the frontend removes it, and the elaboration is
  named;
- **excluded**: not in the IR, with the reason and the exclusion
  category from [design.md](design.md#scope).

Status: not started. The table is produced in step 6, once the corpus
is final. Until then the scope section of the design doc is the
authority.
